# Bounce Bomb
A platformer game made in Godot for the Haven Game Jam!

### Story
You ate too many samosas and now you're too fat to jump anymore. So, you make use of newton's third law: By throwing stuff, stuff throws you.

Throw rocks and propel yourself in the opposite direction to solve the platformer!

Rocks are not enough? Throw bombs in the air. Bombs explode, and they propel you even further than rocks do when they explode near you.

### Controls
**1** and **2**: Switch between rocks and bombs
**A** and **D**: Left & Right movement

### Usage
Visit https://bbunchi.itch.io/bounce-bomb to play the game.

Alternatively, clone the project and export it yourself using godot.
